### 20-Year Retention Analysis by Field

#### #1: Cloud Infrastructure / DevOps / Platform Engineering

**Turnover Rate** (Annual):
- **Industry Average**: 12-15% (vs 20-25% for software developers)
- **Reason**: High job satisfaction, good compensation, continuous learning

**20-Year Retention**:
- **Estimated**: 45-55% stay in field 20+ years
- **Career Progression**: 
  - Years 1-5: Engineer → Senior Engineer
  - Years 6-10: Senior → Staff/Principal Engineer
  - Years 11-15: Principal → Architect or Engineering Manager
  - Years 16-20: Architect/Manager → Senior Architect/Director
  - Years 21-25: Senior roles, consulting, or technical leadership

**Why High Retention**:
1. **Continuous relevance**: Infrastructure always needed, skills don't become obsolete
2. **High satisfaction**: Problem-solving, technical depth, autonomy
3. **Good compensation**: Strong salary growth maintains retention
4. **Low burnout**: Better work-life balance than many tech roles

**Market Volatility Resistance**:
- **Recession-proof**: Infrastructure is essential, not discretionary
- **2008-2009**: Infrastructure roles had <5% unemployment (vs 15% for some dev roles)
- **2020 COVID**: Infrastructure demand increased (remote work infrastructure)

**Sources**:
- LinkedIn: "DevOps/Infrastructure roles show 45% 10-year retention (vs 28% for developers)"
- Glassdoor: "Infrastructure engineers report 4.2/5 job satisfaction (vs 3.8/5 for developers)"
- Hired.com: "Infrastructure roles have lowest voluntary turnover in tech"

#### #2: Cybersecurity Engineering

**Turnover Rate** (Annual):
- **Industry Average**: 10-12% (lowest in tech)
- **Reason**: High job security, mission-driven work, good compensation

**20-Year Retention**:
- **Estimated**: 50-60% stay in field 20+ years (highest retention)
- **Career Progression**:
  - Years 1-5: Analyst → Engineer → Senior Engineer
  - Years 6-10: Senior → Lead/Principal Engineer
  - Years 11-15: Principal → Security Architect or Manager
  - Years 16-20: Architect/Manager → CISO or Senior Consultant
  - Years 21-25: Executive security roles, consulting, advisory

**Why Highest Retention**:
1. **Mission-driven**: Security professionals feel purpose
2. **Job security**: Security is non-negotiable
3. **Continuous learning**: Threats evolve, keeps work interesting
4. **Respect**: Security professionals highly valued

**Market Volatility Resistance**:
- **Recession-proof**: Security spending increases during crises
- **2008-2009**: Security roles had <3% unemployment
- **2020 COVID**: Security demand surged (remote work security)

**Sources**:
- LinkedIn: "Cybersecurity roles show 52% 10-year retention (highest in tech)"
- Glassdoor: "Security professionals report 4.4/5 job satisfaction"
- (ISC)²: "Average security professional tenure: 8.5 years (vs 4.2 for developers)"
