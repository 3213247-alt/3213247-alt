## CAVITIES AND GAPS IN ANALYSIS

### What This Analysis Cannot Guarantee

**Identified Limitations:**

1. **Economic Black Swans**
   - Major recession beyond 2008 severity could disrupt all projections
   - Pandemic-level disruptions unpredictable
   - Source: McKinsey acknowledges 15-20% uncertainty range

2. **AI/Automation Wild Cards**
   - AGI (if achieved) could disrupt ALL knowledge work
   - Current analysis assumes AI remains tool, not replacement
   - Gartner 2025: "15% probability of major paradigm shift in 10 years"

3. **Regulatory Changes**
   - Cybersecurity demand partially driven by compliance requirements
   - Regulatory rollback could reduce demand (low probability but possible)

4. **Geographic Concentration**
   - Best opportunities still in major metros (NYC, DC, SF, Chicago, Austin)
   - Remote work trends could shift this (favorable for entry)

5. **Individual Variation**
   - 84% retention means 16% DO leave
   - Personal factors (burnout, health, family) affect individual outcomes
   - Analysis is probabilistic, not deterministic

### What's Missing from Private Company Reports

**Gaps in Available Data:**

1. **Long-Term Health Outcomes by Field**
   - No private company tracks 25-year health/stress correlation
   - Cybersecurity has documented stress concerns (no quantified data)

2. **Family/Work-Life Balance Tracking**
   - LinkedIn tracks employment, not life satisfaction
   - No private source validates work-life claims

3. **Regional Salary Variation (Complete)**
   - Lightcast covers major metros well
   - Rural/secondary market data incomplete

4. **Career Changer Success Rate (Granular)**
   - "38% entered after 30" doesn't specify success rate by age bracket (30-35, 35-40, etc.)
   - This is a significant gap for this analysis
