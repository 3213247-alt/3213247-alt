## THE BRUTAL TRUTH

### The Real Success Rate Calculation

**Analysis assumes success = "completing training + entering field + staying 20 years"**

**But attrition happens at EVERY stage:**

1. **Training completion:** 70-80% complete (bootcamp dropout ~20-30%)
2. **Landing entry-level role:** 60-70% succeed within 1 year of completing training
3. **Staying 5+ years:** 60% remain (per turnover data)

**Compounded success rate:**
- 75% complete training
- × 65% land role within 1 year  
- × 60% stay 5+ years
- = **29% of people who START achieve 5-year milestone**

**For 20-year retention:**
- 29% reach year 5
- × 60% stay 15 more years (estimated)
- = **~17% of initial entrants stay 20 years**

### What This Means:

**The analysis evaluates outcomes for "MOST PEOPLE WHO SUCCEED"**—not "MOST PEOPLE WHO ATTEMPT"

**Big difference:**
- Most people who complete training AND enter field AND stay 5+ years → achieve projected outcomes ✓
- Most people who START the journey → don't make it (70-80% attrition across all stages) ✗

**You need to be in the 25-30% who persist through all gates to achieve the projected ROI**
