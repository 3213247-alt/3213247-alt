## Methodology

### Data Sources
1. **Primary:** 2010 U.S. Census Surname File (Names_2010Census.csv)
   - Source: census.gov
   - Contains 162,254 surnames with counts, rankings, and demographic breakdowns

2. **Secondary:** UK frequency estimates based on:
   - Ethnic/demographic composition patterns
   - Linguistic origin correlations
   - Known surname distribution patterns between US and UK

### Matching Algorithm
Calculated weighted distance across 4 metrics:
- **US Rank** (30% weight)
- **US Count** (30% weight)  
- **US Per 100k** (30% weight)
- **UK Estimate** (10% weight - lower due to estimation uncertainty)

Lower distance score = closer match to ADLER.
