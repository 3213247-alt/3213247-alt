## Addressing Specific Criteria: Evidence and Rationale

### "People Almost Never Quit That Job" / "Staying for Over 20 Years"
**How This Path Achieves This:**
- **Independent Path:** Consultants who build strong retainer relationships often work with same clients for 15-20+ years. The relationship becomes valuable to both parties.
- **Settled Path:** Knowledge architects who settle into companies often become irreplaceable. Their deep domain knowledge and understanding of the organization's knowledge systems makes them highly valued long-term employees.
- **Why People Stay:**
  - Low stress (no on-call, no production incidents)
  - Intellectual satisfaction without operational pressure
  - Flexibility and independence
  - Growing respect and compensation
  - Deep relationships with clients/colleagues
  - Can take breaks without losing position

### "Little to No Burnout at All" / "Almost Never Burn Out"
**Why This Path Has Low Burnout:**
- **No Operational Stress:** Unlike developers/engineers, no on-call, no production incidents, no urgent fixes
- **Intellectual Work:** Stimulating but not high-pressure
- **Control:** Can control workload, schedule, and projects
- **No Deadlines:** Retainer-based work eliminates deadline pressure
- **Flexibility:** Can take breaks, reduce workload, or pause work entirely
- **Meaningful Work:** Solving real knowledge problems for organizations

### "Relatively Stable Even During Economic Cycles"
**Why This Path is Stable:**
- **Good Times:** Companies need knowledge architecture for growth, expansion, scaling
- **Bad Times:** Companies need knowledge architecture for efficiency, optimization, preserving knowledge
- **Regulatory Needs:** Compliance and regulatory knowledge needs persist regardless of economy
- **AI Integration:** Growing need for knowledge architecture due to AI adoption (not affected by cycles)
- **Essential Function:** Knowledge management is not a "nice to have" - it's essential for organizational function

### "Always Has Opportunities. Even When There Are None."
**Why Opportunities Always Exist:**
- **Internal Needs:** Every company has internal knowledge management challenges
- **Organizational Changes:** Mergers, acquisitions, restructuring all require knowledge architecture
- **Technology Changes:** New technologies create new knowledge architecture needs
- **AI Integration:** Growing need for structuring knowledge for AI systems
- **Regulatory Changes:** New regulations require knowledge system updates
- **Even in "No Opportunities" Environment:** Companies still have internal knowledge problems to solve

### "Plenty of Compensated Days Off if Needed, Up to 1 Year"
**How This Works:**
- **Retainer Structure:** Can negotiate retainer clauses that include paid time off
- **Rate Structure:** In non-mercy labor markets (US, UK), can command rates ($150-$300+/hour) that allow extended time off
