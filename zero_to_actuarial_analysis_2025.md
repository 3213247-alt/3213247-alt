### Question 4: What Foundational Math Skills Are Required Before College-Level Math?

**Analysis**:
- **K-12 Math Sequence**: Arithmetic → Pre-algebra → Algebra I → Geometry → Algebra II → Pre-calculus → Calculus
- **Actuarial Science Prerequisites**: Strong calculus, probability, statistics, linear algebra
- **Gap Assessment**: Starting from "zero" means missing entire K-12 sequence

**Answer**: Required foundational sequence:
1. **Arithmetic** (3-6 months): Fractions, decimals, percentages, basic operations
2. **Pre-Algebra** (3-6 months): Variables, basic equations, number properties
3. **Algebra I** (6-9 months): Linear equations, quadratic equations, functions
4. **Geometry** (6-9 months): Shapes, proofs, trigonometry basics
5. **Algebra II** (6-9 months): Advanced functions, logarithms, complex numbers
6. **Pre-Calculus** (6-9 months): Trigonometry, sequences, limits introduction
7. **Calculus I** (6-9 months): Derivatives, applications
8. **Calculus II** (6-9 months): Integration, series
9. **Calculus III** (6-9 months): Multivariable calculus
10. **Linear Algebra** (3-6 months): Matrices, vector spaces
11. **Probability & Statistics** (6-9 months): Probability theory, statistical inference

**Total Foundation Time**: 4.5-7.5 years if learning sequentially, faster if intensive study (2-3 years full-time equivalent)

**What Needs Clarification**: Learning pace depends on:
- Time available per week (10 hours vs. 40 hours)
- Learning support (tutors, structured programs)
- Natural aptitude for mathematical thinking
- Prior implicit math knowledge from life
