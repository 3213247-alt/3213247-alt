## PRELIMINARY ASSESSMENT

### Likely Accurate Claims:
- Exam pass rates are generally in 40-50% range (widely known)
- Entry-level positions are competitive
- Internships are important for full-time hiring
- Age can be a factor in hiring (though discrimination claims need verification)

### Likely Inaccurate or Unsupported Claims:
- Specific numbers (12,855, 8% growth rate) without sources
- Precise attrition percentages between exams
- Exact success rate percentages (70-80% vs. 20-30%)
- Specific filtering percentages (70% tech skills, 80% communication)

### Needs Further Investigation:
- All specific numbers and percentages
- Age discrimination extent and impact
- Career changer success rates
- Job market projections and methodology
