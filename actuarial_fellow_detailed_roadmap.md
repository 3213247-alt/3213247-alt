## THE EXACT PATH FROM ALBANY

### Phase 1: University at Albany (Years 1-4)

**Major Options:**
1. Mathematics (BA or BS)
2. Applied Mathematics  
3. Economics with Math concentration
4. Statistics (if available)

**Critical Courses:**
- MAT 311: Ordinary Differential Equations
- MAT 362: Probability Theory
- MAT 363: Statistics
- ECO 301: Intermediate Microeconomics
- ECO 302: Intermediate Macroeconomics
- MAT 367: Computational Linear Algebra

**Actuarial Exam Timeline:**
- Sophomore Year: Pass Exam P (Probability)
- Junior Year: Pass Exam FM (Financial Mathematics)
- Senior Year: Pass Exam IFM or STAM

**Internships:**
- Summer after Sophomore: Any insurance company
- Summer after Junior: Actuarial internship
- Companies recruiting from Albany: MetLife, New York Life, Guardian

### Phase 2: Early Career (Years 1-3 Post-Graduation)

**Position:** Actuarial Analyst
**Location:** Remote or hybrid (now standard)
**Salary:** $65,000-85,000 starting
**Focus:** Pass 2-3 more exams, gain specialty

**Exam Progression:**
- Year 1: Pass LTAM or ASTAM
- Year 2: Pass SRM
- Year 3: Pass PA

**Specialization Options:**
- Life Insurance
- Health Insurance
- Property & Casualty
- Pensions
- Enterprise Risk

### Phase 3: Senior Level (Years 4-6)

**Position:** Senior Actuarial Analyst
**Achievement:** Associate of Society of Actuaries (ASA)
**Salary:** $100,000-130,000
**Key:** Build network for future consulting

**Critical Actions:**
- Join specialty track (QFI, ERM, ILA, RET, GI)
- Attend professional conferences
- Write articles for publications
- Build reputation in specialty

### Phase 4: Fellowship Track (Years 7-10)

**Position:** Actuarial Consultant/Manager
**Goal:** Complete Fellowship exams
**Earnings:** $130,000-180,000
**Flexibility:** Already consulting part-time

**Final Exams:**
- 3 Fellowship exams in specialty
- Fellowship modules
- Professionalism course
- **Achievement: FSA or FCAS**

### Phase 5: Independence (Years 10+)

**Position:** Independent Actuarial Consultant
**Structure:** LLC or S-Corp
**Earnings:** $300-800/hour
**Lifestyle:** Complete freedom achieved

## AVOIDING BURNOUT: The Actuarial Advantage

### Why Actuaries Don't Burn Out:

1. **No Emergency Deadlines:**
   - Projections are long-term (5-50 years)
   - Annual valuation cycles predictable
   - No "hair on fire" moments

2. **Intellectual Satisfaction:**
   - Complex problems engaging
   - Continuous learning via exams
   - Respected for expertise

3. **Clear Boundaries:**
   - Work is project-based
   - No 24/7 availability expected
   - Clients respect expertise

4. **Financial Security:**
   - High earnings reduce stress
   - Can afford breaks
   - Not living paycheck to paycheck

### Contrast with Other Fields:

**Investment Banking:** 80-100 hour weeks, deal deadlines
**Law:** Billable hour pressure, court deadlines
**Medicine:** Life/death decisions, night calls
**Tech:** Constant sprints, obsolescence fear
**Consulting:** Travel requirements, client demands

**Actuarial:** Your own schedule, respected expertise

**Demand Drivers:**
- Aging population (pensions/health)

- Regulatory complexity (need experts)
- Emerging risks (AI, pandemic, space)

**Technical Requirements:**

- Secure internet connection
- Video conferencing capability
- That's literally it
