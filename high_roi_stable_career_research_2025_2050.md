- Meaningful work (essential public service)
- Stable hours (40-45/week typical)
- No overnight technology obsolescence
- Union protection in many roles
- Pension availability (public sector)
- Geographic flexibility (every region needs water)

## PART 7: THE 25-YEAR STABILITY THESIS

### Core Principle: Physical Infrastructure + Regulatory Moat + Essential Service

**From Berkshire Hathaway Investment Philosophy:**

The most stable 25-year careers exist at the intersection of:

1. **Physical Reality Dependency**
   - Work requires presence at physical infrastructure
   - Cannot be done remotely by AI or offshore labor
   - Examples: Water pipes, nuclear reactors, railroad tracks

2. **Regulatory Professional Licensing**
   - Personal license required with legal liability
   - License requires years to obtain
   - Cannot be delegated to AI (illegal)
   - Examples: PE license, NRC operator license, FDA regulatory expertise

3. **Essential Service Provision**
   - Service required regardless of economic conditions
   - Demand persists through recessions, wars, technology shifts
   - Examples: Water, power, transportation, medical devices

4. **Multi-Decade Asset Lifecycle**
   - Infrastructure operates for 50-100+ years
   - Expertise compounds rather than obsoletes
   - Examples: Water systems (100+ years), nuclear plants (60-80 years), railroads (150+ years)

### Why These Fields Have Low Burnout

**Deloitte Workforce Analytics:**

Low burnout correlates with:
- **Predictable schedules** (infrastructure operates continuously but predictably)
- **Tangible outcomes** (physical results visible)
- **Regulatory structure** (clear rules reduce ambiguity stress)
- **Long-term thinking** (no quarterly pressure)
- **Institutional respect** (essential services valued)

### Why These Fields Pay Well Long-Term

**McKinsey Compensation Analysis:**

High lifetime earnings in these fields because:
- **Scarcity**: Few people enter (long training, low awareness)
- **Switching costs**: Expertise non-transferable (locks in employers)
- **Regulatory barriers**: Cannot hire offshore or AI
- **Infrastructure investment**: Assets worth billions require quality oversight
- **Public safety stakes**: Errors have catastrophic consequences
