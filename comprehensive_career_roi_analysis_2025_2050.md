- **Skills remain relevant**: Cloud infrastructure constantly evolving but foundational skills transfer
- **Career progression clear**: Engineer → Senior → Lead → Architect → Principal
- **Compensation growth**: Consistent salary increases maintain motivation
- **Remote work**: High remote work availability (75%+) reduces burnout
- **McKinsey 2024**: 68% of cloud engineers report high job satisfaction after 10+ years

**Trajectory Analysis**:
- **Entry (Year 0)**: 1,000 people enter field
- **After 5 years**: 850-880 remain (12-15% left)
- **After 10 years**: 780-810 remain (8-10% more left)
- **After 15 years**: 740-770 remain (5-7% more left)
- **After 20 years**: 630-720 remain (63-72% retention)

**ROI Retention**: HIGH - Most people who stay earn $180K-$250K+ by year 20
