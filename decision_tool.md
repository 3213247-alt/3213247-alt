# Quick Decision Tool: Actuary vs Software Engineer

## 5-Minute Self-Assessment

Answer these questions honestly:

### Math Ability Test
1. Did you score A/A- in Calculus I and II? **YES / NO**
2. Do you enjoy mathematical proofs and theory? **YES / NO**
3. Did you score 650+ on SAT Math (or equivalent)? **YES / NO**

**If 2+ NO → Software Engineer likely better fit**

### Programming Ability Test
1. Have you coded anything (even small projects)? **YES / NO**
2. Do you enjoy debugging and problem-solving? **YES / NO**
3. Can you learn from documentation/Google? **YES / NO**

**If 2+ NO → Actuary might be better fit**

### Discipline Test
1. Can you study 15-20 hours/week for 3+ years? **YES / NO**
2. Have you successfully completed long-term goals (1+ years)? **YES / NO**
3. Are you okay with delayed gratification? **YES / NO**

**If 2+ NO → Software Engineer (faster results)**

### Risk Tolerance Test
1. Are you comfortable with job market uncertainty? **YES / NO**
2. Can you handle the possibility of layoffs? **YES / NO**
3. Do you prefer higher upside over stability? **YES / NO**

**If 2+ NO → Actuary (more stable)**

### Geographic Test
1. Can you relocate to Hartford CT (1.5 hrs from Albany)? **YES / NO**
2. Can you relocate to Des Moines IA or other insurance cities? **YES / NO**
3. Are you willing to commute 1.5+ hours for internship? **YES / NO**

**If 2+ NO → Software Engineer (remote work common)**

### Time Pressure Test
1. Do you need income within 6 months of graduation? **YES / NO**
2. Do you have financial obligations (loans, family)? **YES / NO**
3. Can you afford 2-3 years of job searching/exam prep? **YES / NO**

**If answered YES to Q1 or Q2 → Software Engineer (immediate income)**

## Brutal Truth Matrix

### Actuary Path

**What happens if you succeed** (40% probability):
- Age 30: $100K-$120K salary
- Age 40: $150K-$200K salary
- Age 50: $180K-$250K salary
- Job security: 9/10
- Work-life balance: 8/10
- Career satisfaction: 7/10

**What happens if you fail** (60% probability):
- Stuck at 2-4 exams, can't finish
- Salary plateaus at $85K-$100K
- Wasted 5-7 years on exams
- Must pivot to data analyst, risk management
- Career satisfaction: 4/10

**Expected value**: (0.4 × 9/10) + (0.6 × 4/10) = **6.0/10**

### Software Engineer Path

**What happens if you succeed at FAANG** (15% probability):
- Age 30: $250K-$350K salary
- Age 40: $400K-$500K salary
- Age 50: Retired or moved to management
- Job security: 6/10
- Work-life balance: 5/10
- Career satisfaction: 8/10

**What happens if you're average** (50% probability):
- Age 30: $120K-$150K salary
- Age 40: $140K-$180K salary
- Age 50: $130K-$160K (age discrimination)
- Job security: 5/10
- Work-life balance: 6/10
- Career satisfaction: 6/10

**What happens if you struggle** (35% probability):
- Can't get first job for 12+ months
- Eventual salary: $70K-$90K
- Stuck in low-level roles
- Burnout or layoffs
- Career satisfaction: 4/10

**Expected value**: (0.15 × 8/10) + (0.5 × 6/10) + (0.35 × 4/10) = **5.6/10**

## The 30-Day Commitment Test

**Don't decide now. Do this instead:**

### Week 1-2: Try Both
- Spend 10 hours on Exam P practice problems
- Spend 10 hours coding (Python tutorial + small project)
- Journal: Which one felt more natural? Which did you enjoy?

### Week 3-4: Deep Dive
- Talk to 1 actuary (LinkedIn informational interview)
- Talk to 1 software engineer (LinkedIn informational interview)
- Shadow someone in each field if possible
- Ask: "What do you wish you knew before starting?"

### End of Month: Gut Check
- Which path made you more excited?
- Which path felt more natural?
- Which path aligns with your life goals?

**Decision**: Trust your gut after 30 days of exploration.
