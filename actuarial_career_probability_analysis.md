## PROBABILITY CALCULATION: BASE HIRING LIKELIHOOD

### Scenario 1: Random Selection Model
If all 3,000 qualified candidates have equal chances at 700 entry-level positions:

**Base probability = 700 / 3,000 = 23.3%**

This means in any given year, a qualified candidate has roughly a **23% chance** of securing an entry-level actuarial position.

### Scenario 2: Multi-Year Attempt Probability
If you apply for 2 consecutive years:
- Year 1 failure: 76.7%
- Year 2 attempt available: 76.7%
- Both years failure: 0.767 × 0.767 = 0.588 = 58.8%
- **Success within 2 years: 1 - 0.588 = 41.2%**

If you apply for 3 consecutive years:
- **Success within 3 years: 1 - (0.767)³ = 54.8%**
