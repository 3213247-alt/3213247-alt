## Part 9: The Brutal Truth - Market Saturation

### The Math You Provided

- 2,000 total openings per year
- 700 entry-level openings
- 3,000 qualified candidates per year

**This means**:
```
Surplus candidates = 3,000 - 700 = 2,300 people per year
Surplus rate = 2,300 / 3,000 = 76.67%

77% of qualified candidates don't get entry-level positions each year.
```

### Compounding Effect

If these 2,300 rejected candidates keep applying:
- **Year 1**: 3,000 candidates competing for 700 jobs
- **Year 2**: 2,300 (from Y1) + 3,000 (new) = 5,300 competing for 700 jobs
- **Year 3**: 4,600 (from Y1+Y2) + 3,000 (new) = 7,600 competing for 700 jobs

**However, reality**:
- Many give up after 1-2 years
- Some take non-actuarial jobs and exit candidate pool
- Some eventually get hired in Year 2 or 3

**Steady-state analysis**: If market is truly "constant" at 3,000 candidates and 700 positions, this means:
- 700 new hires per year
- 2,300 exit the candidate pool per year (give up, career change, etc.)

### Your Competition

**Who are the 3,000 qualified candidates?**
1. **Top tier (10%)**: 4+ exams, internships, top universities
   - Hiring probability: 70-90%
2. **Strong tier (20%)**: 3+ exams, internships, good schools
   - Hiring probability: 40-60%
3. **Middle tier (40%)**: 2-3 exams, some experience/internships
   - Hiring probability: 15-25%
4. **Weak tier (30%)**: 1-2 exams, no internships, weaker profiles
   - Hiring probability: 5-10%

**Your position without internship**: Middle to Weak tier
**Your position with internship**: Middle to Strong tier
