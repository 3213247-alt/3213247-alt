# PROBLEM 1: AGE 28 START - AGEISM AVOIDANCE PATH

## The Core Reality

**Starting age**: 28  
**Learning curve duration**: 2-4 years  
**Employment age**: 30-32 years old  
**Mandatory criterion**: Fields where 30+ entry is NORMAL, not penalized

### Step 1: Identify Industries With Documented Age Bias (TO AVOID)

**High Ageism Industries** (per McKinsey Human Capital Reports 2019-2023, Deloitte Workforce Studies):

1. **Technology Startups** - Median employee age: 29
   - Entry after 30 faces implicit bias
   - "Culture fit" often means "young"
   - VC-backed companies show 67% preference for under-30 hires (Deloitte 2022)

2. **Investment Banking (Entry-Level)** - Analyst programs target 22-25
   - Age 30+ entry virtually impossible for analyst roles
   - Associate programs require MBA completion by 28-30

3. **Management Consulting (Entry-Level)** - Similar to IB
   - Post-MBA entry window closes around 32
   - Pre-MBA analyst roles: 22-25 only

4. **Consumer Tech (FAANG entry-level)** - Documented age bias
   - Age discrimination lawsuits (IBM, Google, Amazon)
   - Entry-level engineering roles skew heavily under-30

5. **Advertising/Marketing (Creative roles)** - Youth-obsessed culture
   - Creative director roles: established by early 30s or never

### Step 2: Identify Industries Where 30+ Entry Is NORMAL

**Low Ageism Industries** (per Deloitte Human Capital Trends, McKinsey Industry Reports):

1. **Healthcare (Clinical roles)** - Medical school avg graduation: 26-28
   - Residency completion: 29-34 depending on specialty
   - Nursing: average entry age rising (now 30+)
   - **30+ entry is STANDARD, not exception**

2. **Law** - Law school avg graduation: 27-29
   - Bar passage + employment: 28-31
   - **Career changers common at 30+**

3. **Skilled Trades** - Apprenticeship entry ages broadly distributed
   - Electrician, plumber, HVAC: 25-40 entry common
   - No age ceiling on apprenticeship programs

4. **Insurance (Non-actuarial roles)** - Older workforce industry
   - Average insurance employee age: 44 (Deloitte 2023)
   - Entry at 30 is YOUNG for industry
   - Underwriting, claims, operations: age-neutral hiring

5. **Government/Public Sector** - Age-neutral by design
   - But excluded from this analysis per constraints

6. **Healthcare Administration** - Mid-career entry standard
   - MBA programs average age: 28
   - Hospital administration: entry 28-35 typical

7. **Project Management** - Experience-valued role
   - PMP certification: average age 35+
   - Entry at 30 with prior work experience: advantageous

8. **Cybersecurity** - Skill-based, age-neutral
   - Security clearance careers: 25-50 entry normal
   - Maturity and judgment valued
   - Average practitioner age: 38 (ISACA surveys)

9. **Data Engineering/Analytics** - Later than software engineering
   - Median age higher than general SWE
   - Career transition programs target 28-35 age range

10. **Corporate Finance (Non-IB)** - FP&A, Treasury, Controllership
    - Entry through accounting background: age-neutral
    - CPA + experience: 28-35 entry normal

**OPTIMAL PATHS** (No age penalty, 2-4 year learning curve accepted):

**PATHS TO AVOID** (Age penalty applies at 30+ entry):

| Career Path | Why Avoid |
