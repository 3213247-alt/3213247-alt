### Critical Gaps You Should Know

**Gap 1: This Requires Aptitude for Technical Problem-Solving**
- Cybersecurity requires logical thinking
- Must enjoy puzzles and investigation
- Not suitable for everyone

**Gap 2: Geographic Matters**

**Gap 3: The Learning Never Stops**

**Gap 4: Stress is Part of the Job**
- Security incidents are high-stakes
- On-call requirements common
- Need stress tolerance

**Gap 5: Entry Requires Effort**
- 12-24 months of focused learning
- Must build portfolio/home lab
- Certifications require exam preparation
