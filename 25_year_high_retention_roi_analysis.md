## PART 5: THE DEEP SPECIALIZATION ANALYSIS

### The Hidden Pattern in 20-Year Retention

After analyzing the data, a clear pattern emerges:

**High Retention (70%+) Professions Share These Traits**:

1. **Regulatory Moat** - Government mandates require the role
2. **Physical Infrastructure** - Can't be done remotely or automated
3. **Safety-Critical** - Errors have severe consequences → slow change
4. **Monopolistic/Oligopolistic Industry** - Limited employers but stable
5. **Long Asset Lifecycles** - Equipment/plants last 30-50+ years
6. **Union or Professional Body Protection** - Career pathway protected
7. **Licensing Requirements** - Barrier to entry protects incumbents

**Low Retention (30-50%) Professions Share These Traits**:

1. **Merit-Based Competition** - Constant performance pressure
2. **Rapid Technology Change** - Skills obsolete in 3-5 years
3. **Project-Based Work** - No long-term employment stability
4. **Youth Bias** - Industry favors younger workers
5. **Winner-Take-All Economics** - Few at top, many struggle
6. **Low Switching Costs** - Easy to enter = easy to replace
