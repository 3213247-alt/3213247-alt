#### 2. REINSURANCE ACTUARY (SPECIALTY)

**Retention Data (Source: Swiss Re/Munich Re Workforce Reports)**

- Voluntary turnover: 4.2% annually

From Munich Re Human Capital Report (2022):
- Regular insurance actuary positions declining (automation)
- Reinsurance actuary positions STABLE or GROWING
- Catastrophe modeling requires judgment that cannot be automated
- Treaty negotiation requires relationship management
- Regulatory accountability requires credentialed sign-off

**Stability Factors**
- **Regulatory moat**: Actuarial certification (FSA/FCAS) takes 7-10 years
- **AI resistance**: HIGH - Catastrophe modeling requires judgment on unprecedented events
- **Essential nature**: Insurance industry cannot function without reinsurance
- **Supply constraint**: ~150 qualified reinsurance actuaries enter US market annually

**Burnout Assessment**
- **Work hours**: 40-50 hours/week (less than primary insurance)
- **Stress level**: Moderate (analytical, not customer-facing)
- **Physical demands**: Minimal
- **Mental demands**: High but sustainable (deep analysis, not constant interruption)

**25-Year ROI Calculation**
- **Training**: 7-10 years (bachelor's + exam process while working)
- **Training cost**: $50,000-$120,000 (university + exam fees; often employer reimbursed)
- **Entry salary**: $70,000-$85,000
- **10-year salary**: $150,000-$200,000
- **20-year salary**: $200,000-$350,000
- **Peak salary**: $400,000-$700,000 (Chief Actuary at reinsurer)
- **Lifetime earnings (25 years)**: $5.0M-$8.0M
- **ROI vs training cost**: 40-80x

**AI Disruption Assessment**
- Swiss Re Institute projects actuary roles at reinsurers to remain stable through 2045
- Catastrophe modeling for unprecedented events (climate change, pandemics) requires human judgment
- Regulatory sign-off cannot be automated

**Entry Path**
1. **Bachelor's degree**: Mathematics, Statistics, Actuarial Science (avoid pure actuarial science - too narrow)
2. **Pass 2-3 exams** before/during college
3. **Entry job**: Actuarial analyst at reinsurer or large insurer
4. **Continue exams**: Reach Associate (ASA) in 4-5 years
5. **Specialize**: Move into catastrophe modeling or treaty pricing
6. **Fellowship**: FSA in 7-10 years total
7. **Reinsurer targeting**: Swiss Re, Munich Re, Gen Re, RenaissanceRe, Everest Re, Arch Re

**Key Companies Hiring**
