### Possible Explanations:

1. **Processing Speed Mismatch**

   - Creates appearance of being "rushed" while actually processing methodically

2. **Linear Cognitive Style**
   - Prefers sequential, step-by-step processing
   - Avoids parallel or lateral thinking
   - Focused on direct pathways

3. **Visual Processing Pattern**
   - Minimal eye movement suggests focused attention
   - Forward gaze indicates literal, present-focused processing

4. **Communication Efficiency**
   - Direct, no-nonsense approach
   - Avoids abstraction and metaphor
   - Step-by-step reasoning preferred
