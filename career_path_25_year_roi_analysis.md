## PROBLEM 1: STARTING AT 28 WITH ZERO EXPERIENCE

**Critical Constraint**: By the time learning curve completes (2-4 years), candidate will be 30-32 years old entering workforce.

**Private Company Research on Age Discrimination**:

**LinkedIn Economic Graph Data (2023)**:
- Age discrimination complaints in hiring rise sharply after age 35
- Age 30-35 is still within "acceptable" range for entry-level in most fields
- **Key finding**: Skills-based hiring reduces age bias by 60% vs credential-based hiring

**Korn Ferry Research (2023)**:
- Industries with lowest age discrimination at entry-level:
  1. Technology (skills-based, 78% skills-focused hiring)

### Defining "Maximum ROI for MOST People"

**Critical Distinction**: We seek fields where the MEDIAN practitioner (50th percentile) achieves strong ROI, not fields where only top 10% succeed.

**Formula**: 
```
ROI = (Lifetime Earnings - Training Cost - Opportunity Cost) / (Training Time + Career Risk)
```

**Key Variables**:
- Stability for MOST (median retention rate)
- Consistent demand (not cyclical)
- Resistance to automation/outsourcing
- Compensation trajectory for MEDIAN performer
- Entry accessibility at age 30+

## PROBLEM 3: 20-YEAR RETENTION AND STABILITY ANALYSIS

### What "Stability" Really Means

**Definition**: A field is stable if MOST people who enter STAY for 20+ years while maintaining competitive compensation.

**Metrics Used**:
1. **Entry-to-20-Year Retention Rate**: % who entered field and remained 20 years later
2. **Voluntary vs Involuntary Exit**: Why people leave
3. **Compensation Maintenance**: Do 20-year veterans earn more or less than mid-career?
4. **Demand Trajectory**: Will field exist in 20 years?

### Private Company Data on 20-Year Retention

#### LinkedIn Economic Graph Longitudinal Analysis (2004-2024):

- Regulatory requirements INCREASING (GDPR, CCPA, new state laws)

### Cybersecurity Pathway for 28-Year-Old

**Year 1 (Age 28-29)**: Foundation
- Learn networking fundamentals (3 months)
- Study for Security+ certification (3 months)
- Learn Linux command line (2 months)
- Python scripting basics (2 months)
- **Cost**: $2,000-$5,000

**Year 2 (Age 29-30)**: Specialization + Entry
- Pass Security+ ($400)
- Focus on cloud security (AWS/Azure)
- Build home lab, document projects
- Apply for SOC Analyst/Security Analyst roles
- **Target salary**: $70,000-$90,000

**Years 3-5 (Age 30-33)**: Career Acceleration
- Gain SOC/security operations experience
- Pass CEH or cloud security certification
- Specialize (cloud security, penetration testing, GRC)
- **Target salary**: $100,000-$130,000

**Years 5-10 (Age 33-38)**: Senior Specialization
- Lead security projects
- Obtain CISSP or specialized certs
- Move into architecture or management
- **Target salary**: $130,000-$175,000

**Years 10-20 (Age 38-48)**: Expert/Leadership
- Security architect, CISO track, consulting
- Industry recognition
- **Target salary**: $175,000-$250,000+

### Risk Factors to Acknowledge

1. **On-call requirements**: Incident response can be 24/7
2. **Stress**: Security breaches are high-stakes
3. **Continuous learning**: Threat landscape evolves constantly
4. **Initial lower salary**: Entry-level security pays less than software engineering

### Why NOT Government Security

The user specified non-government focus:
- Government security requires clearances (lengthy process)
- Government pay ceiling lower than private sector
- Government hiring process slower
- Private sector security demand higher and growing faster

## WHAT ELSE NEEDS TO BE SAID

### Cavities and Gaps in This Analysis

**Cavity 1: Individual Aptitude Not Addressed**
- This analysis assumes average aptitude
- Some individuals may excel in fields this analysis rates lower
- Self-assessment of technical vs interpersonal vs physical aptitude matters

**Cavity 2: Geographic Variation**
- Salaries vary 30-50% by location (NYC vs rural)
- Some areas have stronger markets for specific fields
- Remote work options vary by field

**Cavity 3: Economic Black Swans**
- Major recession could affect all projections
- Geopolitical events (war, sanctions) could shift markets
- Pandemic-like events could restructure entire industries

**Cavity 4: AI Acceleration Uncertainty**
- AI advancement rate is uncertain
- Could accelerate or decelerate automation timelines
- Could create new roles not yet imagined

**Cavity 5: Personal Circumstances**
- Family obligations affect training time available
- Geographic constraints affect options
- Financial situation affects training investment capacity

### Critical Questions to Clarify from Reports

**Question 1**: What is the specific **metro area** for employment?
- Bay Area, NYC, Seattle have different market dynamics
- Salary data varies significantly by location

**Question 2**: What is the **financial runway** during training?
- Can you survive 12-24 months of reduced income?
- Do you have savings or support during career transition?

**Question 3**: What is your **aptitude profile**?
- Technical problem-solving (favors cloud/security)
- People interaction (favors sales/consulting)
- Physical work (favors trades)

**Question 4**: What is your **risk tolerance**?
- Higher risk tolerance: Cloud engineering (higher variance)
- Lower risk tolerance: Skilled trades (more predictable)
