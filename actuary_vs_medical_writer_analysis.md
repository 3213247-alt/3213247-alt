## 1. ENTRY REQUIREMENTS & TIME TO FULL QUALIFICATION

### ACTUARY
- **Exams Required:** 7-10 actuarial exams for full credentials (FSA/FCAS)
- **Time to Complete:** 7-10 years while working full-time
- **Study Hours:** 300-400 hours per exam (2,400-4,000 total hours)
- **Pass Rates:** 30-50% per exam
- **Cost:** $4,000-$6,000 in exam fees + study materials
- **Entry Position:** Actuarial Analyst with 1-2 exams passed
- **Fully Qualified:** Age 35-38

### REGULATORY MEDICAL WRITER
- **Additional Education:** None required beyond bachelor's (but MS in life sciences helps)
- **Certifications:** RAC (Regulatory Affairs Certification) optional, takes 6-12 months
- **Time to Entry:** Immediate to 1 year
- **Entry Position:** Junior Medical Writer or Associate
- **Fully Qualified:** Age 30-32
- **Advantage:** 5-6 year head start on career progression

**VERDICT:** Medical writer enters fully productive career 5-6 years earlier. This compounds over 40 years.

## 3. GEOGRAPHIC MOBILITY & ECONOMY-SWITCHING ABILITY

### ACTUARY
- **Country Lock-In:** Exams and credentials are country-specific
  - U.S.: SOA (Society of Actuaries) or CAS (Casualty Actuarial Society)
  - UK: Institute and Faculty of Actuaries
  - Canada: CIA
  - Mutual recognition agreements exist but require additional exams
- **Switching Cost:** 1-3 years to requalify in new country
- **Job Concentration:** Major cities with insurance/financial hubs (NYC, Chicago, Hartford, Boston, Philadelphia)
- **Remote Work:** 40-60% of positions can be remote (post-2025)
- **Mobility Score:** 3/10

### REGULATORY MEDICAL WRITER
- **Country Portability:** Skills transfer directly
  - FDA (U.S.), EMA (Europe), PMDA (Japan), TGA (Australia) all use similar formats
  - ICH (International Council for Harmonisation) standardizes documents globally
- **Switching Cost:** 1-3 months to learn local regulatory nuances
- **Job Concentration:** Biotech/pharma hubs (Boston, San Francisco, New Jersey, San Diego, Research Triangle) + global (Switzerland, UK, Germany, Singapore)
- **Remote Work:** 70-80% of positions can be remote
- **Mobility Score:** 9/10

**CRITICAL DIFFERENCE:** If U.S. economy contracts 2035-2045, actuary is trapped. Medical writer relocates to Germany, Switzerland, UK, Ireland, Singapore within 6 months. This is the **single largest risk differential** between careers.
