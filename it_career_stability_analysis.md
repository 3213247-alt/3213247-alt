# IT Career Progression Analysis: Minimal Regrades & Maximum Employer Respect

## Research Question
What IT career progression:
1. Stands strong in face of adversity (especially employer respect)
2. Outperforms in total "regrades" (career downgrades/demotions) - has the LEAST regrades
3. Works for most people (not just exceptional few)
4. Holds true for next 25 years
5. Compares favorably to actuarial career stability

---

## Step 1: Understanding "Regrades" in Career Context

### Definition of Career Regrades
**Regrade = Career Downgrade/Demotion**, including:
- Forced demotion (title reduction)
- Salary reduction (real or inflation-adjusted)
- Loss of responsibilities
- Age-related discrimination forcing exit
- Skill obsolescence forcing career restart
- Industry disruption eliminating role
- Credential devaluation

### Actuarial Career Regrade Profile (Baseline)
From actuarial analysis:
- **Regrade Rate: VERY LOW**
- Exam-gated progression (clear milestones)
- Credential-based respect (FSA/FCAS permanent)
- Age discrimination: LOW (career viable to 60+)
- Industry disruption: LOW (regulatory protection)
- Skill obsolescence: MEDIUM (must adapt but exams provide structure)
- **Total Regrades Over 40-Year Career: 0-1 typical**

**Why Actuaries Have Low Regrades:**
1. Regulatory requirement (must have actuaries)
2. Credential permanence (FSA/FCAS doesn't expire)
3. Clear progression path (exam milestones)
4. Industry stability (insurance always needed)
5. Professional respect (actuarial opinion carries weight)

## Step 9: Practical Entry Path for IT Audit/Compliance

### Year 1: Foundation
- **Months 1-3**: Learn basic IT concepts (networks, systems, databases)
- **Months 4-6**: Study for Security+ certification (foundational security)
- **Months 7-9**: Study for CISA certification (IT audit focus)
- **Months 10-12**: Pass CISA exam, apply for IT audit roles

### Year 2-3: Entry-Level Role
- **Role**: IT Auditor or IT Compliance Analyst
- **Salary**: $70K-$90K (2025 dollars)
- **Focus**: Learn audit methodologies, compliance frameworks
- **Progress**: Gain experience, understand business processes

### Year 4-5: Mid-Level
- **Role**: Senior IT Auditor or IT Compliance Specialist
- **Salary**: $90K-$120K (2025 dollars)
- **Focus**: Specialize (SOX, PCI-DSS, HIPAA, or industry)
- **Progress**: Consider CISM certification (management focus)
