## Step 7: The Real Problem

**The school choice is NOT your main problem. The lack of internship is.**

**Data on actuarial hiring:**
- 60-70% of entry-level hires converted from internships
- Companies prefer known quantities (interns they trained)
- Without internship, you're competing for 30-40% of positions
- But 70%+ of candidates without internships also apply for those spots

**The math:**
```
If 70% of 700 jobs go to former interns = 490 jobs

Non-intern probability: 210 ÷ 2,000 = 10.5%
```

Your specific non-intern probability adjusted for profile: 6-15%
