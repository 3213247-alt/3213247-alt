# Age 28 Career Entry: Maximum ROI for Most People Over 25 Years (2025-2050)

## CRITICAL CONSTRAINTS ANALYSIS

### Problem 1: Age 28 + Learning Time = Age 30+ Employment (Ageism Risk)

**Reality Check**:
- Age 28 starting point
- Learning period: 6-24 months depending on field
- Employment age: 29-30+ years old
- **Ageism threshold**: Many entry-level positions favor 22-25 year olds
- **Critical window**: Must minimize learning time AND choose fields with age-tolerant hiring

**Ageism Reality by Field** (Private sector hiring data):
- **Software Engineering**: Moderate ageism (30+ seen as disadvantage for entry-level)
- **Data Engineering**: Low ageism (skill-based, 28-32 common entry age)
- **Cybersecurity**: Very low ageism (career changers welcomed, 30-40 common entry)
- **Cloud Engineering**: Low ageism (demand exceeds supply)
- **Actuary**: Moderate ageism (28-30 acceptable, 32+ difficult for entry-level)
- **Sales/Account Management**: No ageism (experience valued)
- **Product Management**: Prefers 30+ (need prior experience)

### Problem 2: Highest ROI for MOST People (Not Elite Few) - 25 Years (2025-2050)

**ROI Definition**:
- Return = Total lifetime earnings - Total investment (time + money)
- Investment = Learning time cost + education costs + opportunity cost
- **CRITICAL**: ROI must apply to **median performers** in field, not just top 10%

**Exclusion Criteria**:
- Fields where only top 10% earn well (example: real estate agents - 90% struggle, 10% get rich)
- Fields requiring elite credentials (example: doctors - only top students get into medical school)
- Fields with winner-takes-all dynamics (example: actors, musicians, entrepreneurs)

**ROI Factors**:
1. **Median salary** (not average - average skewed by outliers)
2. **Salary floor** (what bottom 25% earns)
3. **Job availability** (can median performers find jobs?)
4. **Learning time** (shorter = better ROI)
5. **Career longevity** (25-year demand stability)
6. **Entry age tolerance** (critical for age 30+ entry)

- Software Engineering: MODERATE-HIGH automation risk (AI code generation)
