## CAVITY CATEGORY 1: Personal Situation Unknowns (CRITICAL)

### **Gap 1.1: Financial Situation**

**What's Missing**:
- Current savings available for education
- Existing debt obligations
- Monthly expenses and financial obligations
- Ability to study full-time vs must work during learning
- Access to loans or family support

**Why It Matters**:
- Determines viable education paths (trade school vs bootcamp vs degree)
- Affects timeline (full-time study vs part-time)
- Influences risk tolerance for career choice

**Impact on Recommendation**:
- High savings → Can pursue longer education paths (nursing ADN, extended bootcamp)
- Low savings → Must prioritize fastest path with income during training (trades apprenticeship)
- Debt burden → Must prioritize highest early earnings potential
- Must work → Eliminates full-time programs, extends timeline

**Status**: ⚠️ CRITICAL GAP - Cannot provide precise recommendation without this
