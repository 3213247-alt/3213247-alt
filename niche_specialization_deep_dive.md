## WATER INFRASTRUCTURE ENGINEERING - NICHE SPECIALIZATIONS

### 1. Dam Safety Engineering

**From Black & Veatch & AECOM Reports:**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| U.S. Licensed Dam Safety Engineers | ~2,500 |
| Dams Requiring Inspection | 91,000+ |
| Average Age of Dam Engineers | 54 years |
| Projected Retirements (10 years) | 45% of workforce |
| AI Displacement Risk | 0.3/10 |

**Why Extreme Stability:**
- Federal law requires licensed engineer sign-off on dam safety
- Physical inspection cannot be automated (legal liability)
- Most dams built 1930-1970, all need assessment
- Catastrophic failure consequences prevent AI delegation
- Only 25 universities offer dam safety programs

**Salary Range:**
- Entry: $70K-$85K
- Mid-Career: $110K-$150K
- Senior: $150K-$220K

**Entry Path:**
1. Civil Engineering + Geotechnical focus
2. PE license
3. 3-5 years at dam owner (utility, water authority)
4. Dam safety certification (ASDSO)

## CONCLUSION

The most stable 25-year career niches share common characteristics:
1. **Physical infrastructure dependency** - Cannot be digitized
2. **Personal licensing with liability** - Cannot delegate to AI
3. **Aging workforce** - Retirements create openings
4. **Regulatory mandate** - Law requires the work
5. **Essential service** - Society cannot function without it

**For maximum stability**: Choose a niche within infrastructure engineering that combines aging workforce + regulatory mandate + physical inspection requirements.

**Top recommendation for age 28**: Dam Safety Engineering or Water Treatment Process Engineering - accessible entry, growing demand, extreme stability, and 25-year clear runway.
