## PROBLEM 1: Ageism Avoidance for 28+ Career Entry

### The Core Issue

Starting at 28 with a learning curve of 2-4 years means employment at age 30-32. This creates real ageism risks in certain fields.

### Fields WITH Significant Age Bias (AVOID)

**1. Software Engineering at FAANG/Big Tech (Entry-Level)**
- **Reality**: Median age at Meta is 28, Google is 30 (disclosed in company diversity reports)
- **Problem**: At 32+ entering as junior, you compete with 22-year-olds who have same technical skills
- **Ageism Evidence**: Age discrimination lawsuits against Google, IBM, HP (public court records)
- **Source**: Hired.com salary reports, company SEC filings, court documents

**2. Investment Banking Analyst Programs**
- **Reality**: 22-25 age range is expected for analyst class
- **Problem**: 30+ at entry is considered "too old" for 80-hour weeks
- **Source**: Wall Street Oasis industry surveys (private), Glassdoor compensation data

**3. Management Consulting (MBB Entry)**
- **Reality**: Post-MBA associate is expected at 28-30
- **Problem**: At 32+ without MBA, direct hire is nearly impossible
- **Source**: Consulting firm annual reports, Glassdoor data, Management Consulted (private firm)

**4. Startups (Entry-Level IC Roles)**
- **Reality**: Startup culture favors young workers willing to accept equity over salary
- **Problem**: Age 30+ workers have families/mortgages, less willing to take risk
- **Source**: First Round Capital State of Startups reports (private VC firm)

### Fields WITHOUT Significant Age Bias (TARGET THESE)

**1. Insurance Industry (Non-Actuarial Roles)**
- **Why**: Older workforce, mature industry, values experience
- **Median Age**: 42-45 across major insurers
- **Entry Age Tolerance**: 30+ is common and accepted
- **Source**: Insurance industry hiring reports (Jacobson Group - private staffing firm)

**2. Healthcare Administration**
- **Why**: Second-career professionals are common
- **Median Age**: 44 in healthcare administration
- **Entry Age Tolerance**: 30+ is normal, experience valued
- **Source**: MGMA compensation data (private association), Becker's Healthcare reports

**3. Utilities and Energy**
- **Why**: Aging workforce, desperate for new talent
- **Median Age**: 45+ at most utilities
- **Entry Age Tolerance**: 30+ welcomed, often preferred
- **Source**: Utility industry compensation surveys (private consultancies)

**4. Government Contracting/Defense Industry**
- **Why**: Security clearance process favors stable adults
- **Median Age**: 44-48 across defense contractors
- **Entry Age Tolerance**: 30+ is advantageous (perceived maturity)
- **Source**: Defense contractor annual reports, ClearanceJobs.com (private)

**5. Corporate Finance/FP&A (Non-IB)**
- **Why**: Values experience and judgment
- **Entry Age Tolerance**: 30+ is common
- **Source**: Robert Half salary guides (private staffing firm)

### Key Insight for Problem 1

**The 28-start/30-32-employment scenario is NOT a problem if you target the right industries.**

**Target Profile**:
- Industries with median worker age 40+
- Companies with stable, predictable business models
- Roles where experience and judgment matter more than speed
- Avoid youth-obsessed tech culture
