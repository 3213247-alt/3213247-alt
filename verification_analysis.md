## CLAIM 3: Attrition Rates Between Exams

### Claims Made:
- 60% continue from Exam P to FM
- 70% continue from 2 exams to 3 exams
- 60% continue from 3 exams to 4 exams

### Verification Status: **CANNOT VERIFY - ESTIMATED**

**What We Know:**
- SOA/CAS do NOT publish detailed attrition data between exams
- This data would require tracking individual candidates across multiple exams
- Attrition is inferred from exam volume differences

**Assessment:**
- **Accuracy**: UNKNOWN - These appear to be ESTIMATES
- No public source tracks individual candidate progression
- Attrition rates are likely inferred from:
  - Comparing Exam P volume to Exam FM volume
  - Comparing Exam FM volume to Exam 3 volume
  - But this doesn't account for:
    - People taking exams out of order
    - People retaking exams
    - People switching between SOA and CAS tracks

**Critical Issue:**
- **These attrition rates are likely OVERESTIMATED**
- Many candidates take exams out of sequence
- Many candidates retake failed exams
- The calculation assumes linear progression, which is not accurate

**Reality Check:**
- If 12,855 take Exam P and 48% pass = 6,170 pass
- But many who fail retake Exam P
- Many who pass Exam P may not immediately take FM
- The "60% continue" assumption is likely flawed
