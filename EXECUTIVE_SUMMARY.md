### 1. Role Selection
- **Cloud Architect**: Best work-life balance (40-45 hours, minimal on-call)
- **Cloud Security Engineer**: Best balance of compensation + work-life balance
- **DevOps Engineer**: More hands-on, moderate on-call

### 2. Industry Selection
- **Finance**: Highest compensation (+15-25%), highest stability (75% retention)
- **Healthcare**: Good compensation (+5-10%), highest stability (72% retention)

- **Manufacturing**: Lower compensation (-5-10%), high stability (70% retention)

### 3. Learning Scenario
- **Full-Time**: Fastest (12-18 months), requires $30K-$60K savings
- **Part-Time**: Balanced (18-24 months), allows income during learning
- **Employer-Sponsored**: Lowest risk (18-24 months), employer covers costs
