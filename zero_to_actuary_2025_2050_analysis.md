### Factor 1: Exam Progression Risk

**SOA (Society of Actuaries) Candidate Database Analysis (anonymized data shared with employers, 2024):**

**Exam Pass Rates:**
- Exam P (Probability): 47% pass rate
- Exam FM (Financial Math): 49% pass rate
- Exam IFM (Investment/Financial Markets): 42% pass rate

**Cumulative Risk:**
- Probability of passing 4 exams in a row: 0.47 × 0.49 × 0.42 × 0.38 = 3.6%
- **Reality**: Most people fail 1-2 times per exam
- Average attempts per exam: 1.8-2.2
- **Time impact**: Each exam failure adds 6 months

  4. Financial strain (10%)

**Starting at Age 34 Risk:**
- Less tolerance for exam failures
- Family obligations may interfere with study (300+ hours per exam)
- Financial pressure (student loans, living costs)
