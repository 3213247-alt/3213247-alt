## PROBLEM 1: AGEISM-RESISTANT PATH ANALYSIS
### Starting at Age 28, Avoiding Age Discrimination

**Core Challenge**: 
- Learning curve takes 2-3 years minimum
- Person will be 30-31+ by time of employment
- Must avoid fields with age discrimination patterns

**Ageism Patterns in US Labor Market (Private Industry Analysis)**:

**High Ageism Risk Fields** (AVOID):
1. **Entry-level Software Development** - Age 30+ entry faces discrimination
2. **Social Media Marketing** - Youth-oriented hiring bias
3. **Gaming Industry** - Strong age bias documented
4. **Startup Culture Roles** - Preference for younger workers
5. **Fashion/Entertainment Tech** - Age-conscious industries

**Low Ageism Risk Fields** (PREFERRED):
1. **Professional Services** - Age = experience = credibility
2. **Regulatory/Compliance** - Maturity valued
3. **Risk Management** - Experience premium
4. **Healthcare Analytics** - Age diversity common
5. **Financial Services (Non-Trading)** - Age-neutral to positive
6. **Insurance Industry** - Actuarial, underwriting value experience
7. **Cybersecurity (Enterprise)** - Experience highly valued
8. **Data Engineering (Enterprise)** - Maturity in handling complex systems

**Critical Insight**: Fields requiring **judgment, regulatory knowledge, and stakeholder management** show minimal ageism. Fields requiring **trend awareness, cultural fit, or physical stamina** show higher ageism.

**Recommended Path Characteristics**:
- Professional credential-based (not degree-based)
- Experience-weighted compensation
- Regulatory or compliance component
- Client-facing or stakeholder management
- Industry with established career ladders
