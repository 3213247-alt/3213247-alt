# PRIVATE COMPANY SOURCES TO FIND - Quick Reference

**Purpose**: List of specific private company reports needed to validate career recommendation and fill data gaps

---

## TIER 1: MUST-HAVE REPORTS (Healthcare Focus)

### Deloitte (Private Consulting Firm)

**Report 1: "2025 Global Health Care Outlook"**
- **Where**: deloitte.com/global/en/industries/health-care.html
- **Access**: Free executive summary, full report may require registration
- **What to look for**:
  - Nursing workforce demand projections 2025-2050
  - NP market growth and saturation analysis
  - Healthcare employment stability data
  - Age diversity trends in healthcare

**Report 2: "The Future of Nursing Workforce"** or similar title
- **Where**: Search deloitte.com for "nursing workforce"
- **Access**: May require contacting Deloitte Health consulting division
- **What to look for**:
  - ABSN program outcomes
  - Retention rates by nursing specialty
  - Career advancement data (RN to NP transition rates)

### McKinsey & Company (Private Consulting Firm)

**Report 1: "The Future of Healthcare Workforce"** or "Healthcare Labor Market"
- **Where**: mckinsey.com → Industries → Healthcare → Our Insights
- **Access**: Free articles and reports on website
- **What to look for**:
  - Healthcare employment projections through 2040-2050
  - Nurse practitioner demand analysis
  - Retention and turnover data
  - Age demographics in healthcare professions

**Report 2: "Addressing the Future of Healthcare Labor Shortages"**
- **Where**: Search mckinsey.com for "nursing shortage" or "healthcare workforce"
- **Access**: Free
- **What to look for**:
  - Supply vs demand for nurses and NPs
  - Geographic distribution of opportunities
  - Salary and compensation trends

### PwC Health Research Institute (Private Consulting Firm)

**Report 1: "Top Health Industry Issues" (Annual Report)**
- **Where**: pwc.com/us/en/industries/health-industries/library.html
- **Access**: Free annual report
- **What to look for**:
  - Workforce sections
  - Nursing and advanced practice trends
  - Healthcare employment outlook
  - Value-based care impact on workforce

**Report 2: "Medical Cost Trend: Behind the Numbers" (Annual)**
- **Where**: pwc.com → Health Research Institute
- **Access**: Free
- **What to look for**:
  - Healthcare spending projections (indicates job stability)
  - Service line growth areas
  - Provider workforce trends

### Advisory Board (Private Healthcare Consulting)

**Report 1: "Nursing Workforce Compendium"**
- **Where**: advisory.com → Search "nursing workforce"
- **Access**: Some content free, full access may require membership
- **What to look for**:
  - Detailed retention data
  - Career trajectory analysis
  - ABSN program outcomes
  - Age diversity in nursing education

## HOW TO ACCESS REPORTS

### Free Access Methods:

1. **Direct Website Downloads**
   - Most consulting firms publish free executive summaries and selected full reports
   - Visit firm websites → Industries → Healthcare or Future of Work sections
   - Register with email (free) for access to reports

2. **LinkedIn Research**
   - Follow consulting firm LinkedIn pages
   - Often share reports and insights for free
   - Can contact consultants directly for report access

3. **Google Search**
   - Search: "[Consulting Firm] nursing workforce report PDF"
   - Many reports available as direct PDF downloads

4. **Business School Libraries**
   - If you have university access, business school libraries often have consulting firm report databases
   - Ask librarians for help accessing Deloitte, McKinsey, BCG, PwC reports

### Paid Access Methods (If Needed):

1. **Direct Purchase**
   - Full reports: $500-$2,000 typically
   - Contact consulting firm research divisions
   - Often can purchase individual reports

2. **Subscription Databases**
   - Gartner, Forrester require subscriptions ($5,000-$50,000/year)
   - Not recommended for individual research
   - Access through employer or library if possible

3. **Consulting Firm Engagement**
   - Some firms provide reports free to potential clients
   - Schedule consultation call to discuss your career decision
   - May gain access to relevant research

### Networking Methods:

1. **LinkedIn Connections**
   - Connect with healthcare consultants at Deloitte, McKinsey, PwC
   - Ask if they can share relevant workforce reports
   - Many consultants willing to help with career decisions

2. **Professional Associations**
   - Nursing associations may have access to consulting firm reports
   - Healthcare executive associations often commission studies

3. **University Career Centers**
   - Career counselors may have access to industry reports
   - Some universities subscribe to consulting firm databases

## WHAT TO LOOK FOR IN EACH REPORT

### Healthcare Workforce Reports - Key Data Points:

1. **Age Diversity**
   - What % of nursing students are 28+ years old?
   - What % of ABSN students vs traditional BSN?
   - Success rates by age cohort

2. **Retention Rates**
   - 5-year, 10-year, 20-year retention for RNs
   - NP retention rates (should be higher than RN)
   - Reasons for leaving (burnout vs career advancement vs retirement)

3. **Supply vs Demand**
   - Nursing shortage projections 2025-2050
   - NP market saturation risk analysis
   - Geographic variation (which states have strongest demand)

4. **ROI and Compensation**
   - Starting salaries for RNs and NPs
   - Mid-career salary progression
   - Lifetime earnings estimates
   - Education cost vs earnings analysis

5. **Career Pathways**
   - What % of RNs pursue advanced practice (NP, CRNA, etc.)?
   - Timeline from RN to NP
   - Success rates for part-time NP programs

6. **Future Outlook**
   - Impact of aging population (baby boomers)
   - Technology/AI impact on nursing work
   - Policy changes (healthcare reform, NP scope of practice)

### Technology Workforce Reports - Key Data Points:

1. **Age Discrimination**
   - Quantification of ageism in tech hiring
   - Average age of employees at tech companies
   - Age at which tech workers face employment challenges

2. **Alternative Pathways**
   - Bootcamp graduate success rates by age
   - Self-taught programmer employment rates
   - Career changer success in tech

3. **Retention**
   - How many tech workers stay in tech for 20+ years?
   - At what age do tech workers typically transition out?
   - Where do they go (management, other industries, etc.)?

### Workforce/Age Diversity Reports - Key Data Points:

1. **Career Starter Success by Age**
   - Employment rates for career starters at 28, 30, 35, 40
   - Fields most friendly to age-diverse entry
   - Fields with highest age discrimination

2. **ROI by Career Change Age**
   - Return on education investment for 28+ year olds
   - Comparison across professions
   - Time to positive ROI

## RED FLAGS TO WATCH FOR

### In Healthcare Reports:

❌ **NP market saturation warnings** - Too many NP programs opening
❌ **Reimbursement cuts** - Medicare/Medicaid payment reductions
❌ **Scope of practice restrictions** - States limiting NP autonomy
❌ **Burnout crisis worsening** - Retention declining significantly

### In Technology Reports:

❌ **Age discrimination increasing** - Tech companies reducing age diversity
❌ **Bootcamp failure rates rising** - Alternative pathway success declining
❌ **Automation acceleration** - Software engineering roles at high AI risk

### In Workforce Reports:

❌ **Career changer success declining** - Employers preferring traditional candidates
❌ **Age 28+ employment rates dropping** - Ageism worsening across industries

### If These Red Flags Appear:

- **Healthcare red flags**: Consider electrician apprenticeship instead
- **Tech red flags**: Eliminate tech paths, focus on healthcare or trades
- **General workforce red flags**: Prioritize fields with proven age-diverse hiring

---

## GREEN FLAGS TO LOOK FOR

### In Healthcare Reports:

✅ **Growing nursing shortage** - Demand exceeds supply
✅ **NP scope expansion** - More states granting full practice authority
✅ **Aging population emphasis** - Demographic trends favor healthcare growth
✅ **ABSN program success** - High graduation and employment rates
✅ **Age-diverse student bodies** - 30+ year olds common in nursing programs

### In Workforce Reports:

✅ **Healthcare ranks as age-friendly** - Low discrimination compared to other fields
✅ **Career changers succeeding** - High success rates for 28+ year old career starters
✅ **Long-term retention high** - People stay in field for 20+ years

### If These Green Flags Appear:

**VALIDATION** - Proceed with confidence in RN→NP recommendation
