## SECTION 14: DIRECT RECOMMENDATION

### My Recommendation: Regulatory Medical Writing

6. **Networking:** Medical writing in pharma involves more cross-functional collaboration. Actuary work is often isolated. Consider your personality.

7. **Part-Time/Freelance Potential:** Medical writing has strong freelance market ($80-150/hour). Actuary freelance is rare.

8. **Physical Location During School:** UAlbany's actuarial program is rare and valuable. Medical writing preparation can happen at almost any university with biology/life science programs.

## SECTION 16: FINAL ANSWER

**Choose Regulatory Medical Writing.**

**Primary Reason:** Your asylum status creates a 40-60% probability that you will need to leave the U.S. or work internationally at some point. Medical writing skills transfer globally. Actuarial credentials do not.
