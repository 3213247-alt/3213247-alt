## STEP 1: AGEISM ANALYSIS - FIELDS TO AVOID

### Ageism Risk Assessment Framework

**High Ageism Risk Fields** (AVOID):
1. **Entry-level software engineering** - Age 30+ entry severely disadvantaged
2. **Investment banking analyst programs** - Explicitly targets 22-25 year olds
3. **Management consulting entry-level** - Prefers recent graduates
4. **Tech startups (general roles)** - Cultural bias toward youth
5. **Gaming industry development** - Age discrimination documented
6. **Social media marketing** - Perceived as "young person's field"

**Medium Ageism Risk Fields** (CAUTION):
1. **Data science (entry-level)** - Some ageism, but skills can overcome
2. **Product management (entry-level)** - Varies by company
3. **Digital marketing (entry-level)** - Depends on specialization

**Low Ageism Risk Fields** (SAFE):
1. **Cybersecurity** - Experience valued, age-agnostic
2. **Cloud architecture** - Skills-based, not age-based
3. **DevOps/Platform engineering** - Experience premium
4. **Healthcare IT** - Age diversity common
5. **Financial services (non-IB)** - Experience valued
6. **Insurance technology** - Age diversity
7. **Regulatory compliance** - Experience critical
8. **Risk management** - Age and experience advantage
9. **Technical consulting** - Expertise over age
10. **Specialized technical roles** - Skills trump age

**Critical Insight**: Fields requiring **certifications, credentials, or specialized expertise** show less ageism because they're skills-based rather than culture-based.
