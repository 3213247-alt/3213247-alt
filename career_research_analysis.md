## Problem 1: Ageism Avoidance Strategy

### Key Considerations:
- **Starting Age**: 28 years old
- **Learning Curve**: 2-3 years minimum
- **Employment Age**: 30+ years old
- **Critical**: Must avoid fields with significant age bias

### Fields to AVOID (High Ageism Risk):
1. **Consumer Tech Startups** - Strong bias toward younger workers
2. **Gaming Industry** - Age discrimination well-documented
3. **Social Media Companies** - Youth-focused culture
4. **Fashion/Retail Tech** - Age-based hiring preferences
5. **Entry-Level Software Development** - Competitive with recent graduates

### Fields FAVORABLE for 30+ Entry:
1. **Healthcare Technology** - Values maturity and life experience
2. **Financial Services Technology** - Age seen as asset (trust, stability)
3. **Enterprise Software** - Experience valued over youth
4. **Cybersecurity** - Maturity and judgment highly valued
5. **Regulatory/Compliance Technology** - Age and experience advantages
6. **Industrial/Manufacturing Tech** - Values practical experience
7. **Healthcare Administration** - Age-neutral to positive
8. **Project Management** - Life experience is asset
9. **Business Analysis** - Analytical maturity valued
10. **Data Engineering/Analytics** - Experience with business context valued

- ✅ Predictable hours
- ✅ Low burnout rates
- ✅ Deep, wide, fascinating learning curve
- ✅ Stable even in extreme labor market conditions

- **Hours**: Standard business hours, minimal overtime
