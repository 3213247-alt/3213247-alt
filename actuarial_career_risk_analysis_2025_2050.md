## Step 1: Current Actuarial Career Requirements (2025 Baseline)

### Traditional Actuarial Path Structure
1. **Mathematical Foundation Required**
   - University-level calculus (2-3 semesters)
   - Probability and statistics (2-3 semesters)
   - Linear algebra
   - Time to acquire: 2-3 years of full-time study minimum

2. **Actuarial Exam Series**
   - SOA (Society of Actuaries): 5-10 exams over 5-10 years
   - CAS (Casualty Actuarial Society): Similar structure
   - Each exam: 3-6 months study time
   - Cannot be accelerated significantly

3. **Professional Timeline**
   - Entry-level: 2-3 exams passed (1-2 years)
   - Associate (ASA/ACAS): 5-7 exams (3-5 years)
   - Fellowship (FSA/FCAS): 8-10 exams (5-10 years)

4. **Starting from Zero Math in 2025**
   - Years 1-3: Build mathematical foundation
   - Years 3-8: Pass actuarial exams while working
   - Years 8-15: Reach Fellowship level
   - **Total time to full qualification: 8-15 years from zero**

**Analysis:**
- Starting from zero math in 2025 requires 2-3 years of mathematical education
- This is a significant time and financial investment
- By the time this foundation is built (2027-2028), AI will have advanced further
- The investment becomes riskier as AI progresses during the learning period

**Step-by-Step Logic:**
1. Invest 2-3 years in math foundation (2025-2028)
2. AI continues advancing during this period
3. Market conditions worsen during learning period
4. Investment may not pay off if market collapses
5. **Risk: High upfront investment with uncertain return**

### Point 2: Exam-Based Qualification Loses Value

**Analysis:**
- Actuarial exams test knowledge that AI systems can perform
- Companies value AI/ML skills over exam credentials
- Exam-based progression becomes less relevant
