# Why Other Paths Fall Short: A Critical Analysis

## Paths That Seem Perfect But Aren't

### 1. Data Science (Including at UAlbany)

**Why it seems to fit:**
- Continuous learning (new algorithms, tools)
- Remote work common
- International mobility
- Good salaries

**Why it actually doesn't:**
- **Too broad**: "Data Scientist" means 100 different things
- **Automation risk**: AutoML tools replacing junior data scientists
- **Respect problem**: Everyone claims to be a "data scientist" now
- **No standardized progression**: Unlike actuarial exams or epidemiology certifications
- **Burnout**: Constantly chasing latest technology
- **Limited depth**: Jack of all trades, master of none

**The killer flaw**: After 10 years, a data scientist might know 50 tools superficially, while an epidemiologist deeply understands causal inference.
