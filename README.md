**PROS**:
- Age-friendly (all ages accepted)

- Low education cost ($0-$10K)
- Cannot outsource, low automation risk
- Business ownership potential

**CONS**:
- Physical demands (career may end at age 55-60)
- Lower income as employee ($55K-$75K journeyman)
- Economic cycle sensitivity

**ROI**: Very strong (low cost, good earnings, business potential)

**When to choose**: If physically capable and interested in business ownership

**PROS**:
- Fast timeline (12-18 months)
- High income potential ($100K-$150K mid-career)
- Remote work options

**CONS**:
- Tech industry ageism (especially after age 45)
- Highly competitive entry
- Lower retention (50-65% over 20 years)
- Automation risk

**ROI**: High but risky

**When to choose**: If strong technical aptitude and plan to pivot to management by age 45

**PROS**:

- Stable demand
- High retention (75-85%)

**CONS**:
- High education cost ($80K-$150K)
- Moderate income ($75K-$100K)
- Lower ROI than NP

**ROI**: Moderate

**When to choose**: If passionate about physical rehabilitation (but NP is financially superior)
