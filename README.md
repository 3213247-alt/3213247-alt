# Career Research Repository

## Research on High-ROI Career Fields with Long-Term Stability

This repository contains comprehensive research on career paths with the highest return on investment (ROI) over a 25-year time horizon, focusing on:

- Industries with multi-decade stable cashflows
- Fields with 20+ year retention rates
- Career paths resistant to AI disruption
- Specialized niches with high stability and low burnout

## Main Document

**[CAREER_RESEARCH_ANALYSIS.md](CAREER_RESEARCH_ANALYSIS.md)** - Complete analysis with actionable recommendations

## Research Methodology

Analysis based exclusively on private sector sources:
- Management consulting firm reports (McKinsey, BCG, Deloitte, PwC)
- Private company financial disclosures
- Industry trade associations
- Professional organizations
- Corporate investor presentations

Government sources, labor statistics, and educational institution reports were excluded per research parameters.

## Key Findings

**Top 3 Recommendations for Age 28, 25-Year Career:**

1. **Actuarial Science (Specialized)** - Highest ROI, 82% 20+ year retention
2. **Medical Device Field Service Engineering** - Healthcare stability, hands-on + cognitive
3. **Industrial Reliability Engineering** - Essential manufacturing, compound expertise
