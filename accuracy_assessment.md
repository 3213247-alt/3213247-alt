## Critical Issues Found

### Issue 1: Circular Reasoning
The analysis calculates how many people achieve the "full profile" (3-4 exams + tech + communication), then assumes only these people can get jobs. But in reality:
- Many jobs go to candidates with 1-2 exams + strong internship
- Some go to candidates with fewer exams but better networking
- Geographic flexibility matters more than exam count for some positions

**Impact:** Overstates exclusivity and understates actual job opportunities

---

### Issue 2: Missing Success Factors
Analysis focuses on exams + tech + communication but ignores:
1. **Geographic flexibility** (HUGE factor - willing to relocate?)
2. **Networking** (connections matter enormously)
3. **Internship quality** (which company? what projects?)
4. **Timing** (when are you in job market?)
5. **Interview skills** (can you communicate your value?)
6. **Company preferences** (some prefer 2 exams + internship over 4 exams)

**Impact:** Oversimplifies hiring process

---

### Issue 3: Age Discrimination Overstatement
Claims age 34+ is "automatic filter" reducing success to 20-30%.

**Reality:**
- Age discrimination exists but is not absolute
- Many successful career changers in actuarial field
- Success depends more on: credentials, networking, persistence, geographic flexibility
- Age 34 is below ADEA protection (40+), but many companies don't discriminate at 34

**Evidence Gap:**
- No research cited
- No data on age-based success rates
- Appears to be speculation

**Impact:** May unnecessarily discourage qualified older candidates

---

### Issue 4: Success Rate Definition Unclear
"Success rate" is never clearly defined:
