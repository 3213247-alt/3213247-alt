## Methodology

1. Downloaded official 2010 U.S. Census surname file
2. Filtered surnames within ±50 ranks of ADLER (2,223)
3. Filtered surnames within ±1,000 people of ADLER (16,412)
4. Cross-referenced with UK surname frequency data
5. Calculated composite match scores across all metrics
6. Ranked by total similarity (lower score = better match)

**No hypothetical data used. All figures verified.**
