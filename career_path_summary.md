## Career Path Timeline

### Phase 1: Foundation (Years 1-3)
- **Starting Point:** Bachelor's degree + 2-5 years technical experience
- **Activities:** Work in technical role, begin knowledge management, study knowledge architecture, build portfolio
- **Education:** Knowledge Management certifications, technical writing, information architecture, content strategy
- **Outcome:** Ready to transition

### Phase 2: Transition (Years 3-5)
- **Activities:** Take on knowledge architecture projects (part-time/freelance), build reputation, develop methodology, build network
- **Education:** Deepen knowledge architecture expertise, learn AI/knowledge systems integration, develop specialization
- **Outcome:** Established as knowledge architecture consultant

### Phase 3: Established Professional (Years 5-10)
- **Activities:** Full-time knowledge architecture consulting, multiple clients or long-term retainers, highly respected, work from anywhere, take extended time off
- **Characteristics:** High demand, premium rates, low burnout, stable income, global opportunities
- **Outcome:** Established, respected, independent knowledge architecture consultant

### Phase 4: Senior Expert (Years 10-20+)
- **Activities:** Recognized expert, choose projects selectively, work with same companies for decades, take extended sabbaticals, may mentor or build team, can settle into one company or continue independent
- **Characteristics:** Very high respect, scarcity increases, deep domain knowledge becomes irreplaceable, companies invest in long-term relationships, work less while earning more
- **Outcome:** Senior expert with complete flexibility and independence
