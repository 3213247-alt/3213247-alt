## HYBRID PATH (ADVANCED OPTION)

**Can you do BOTH? Maybe.**

**Option 1: Cyber first, NP later**
1. Age 28-30: Get into cybersecurity (18 months)
2. Age 30-35: Work cybersecurity, save $100K-$150K, pay off any debts
3. Age 35-40: Do accelerated NP program (2-3 years), pay cash, no loans
4. Age 40-65: Work as NP with better work-life balance

**Pros:**
- ✓ Generate income fast (cybersecurity)
- ✓ Pay for NP school in cash (no debt)
- ✓ End career in better work-life balance role (NP)
- ✓ Cybersecurity experience = better understanding of healthcare IT (valuable specialization)

**Cons:**
- ✗ Very long path (12 years before NP career starts)
- ✗ Requires discipline to save money during cyber years
- ✗ Two separate learning curves

**Verdict**: This actually works well. Cyber → NP is a smart sequence.

**Option 2: NP with cybersecurity side skills**
1. Age 28-33: Become NP (5 years)
2. Age 33-35: Work as NP, learn health IT/cybersecurity on side
3. Age 35+: Pivot to healthcare cybersecurity (hospitals, health tech companies)

**Pros:**
- ✓ Healthcare + cybersecurity = rare, valuable combination
- ✓ Healthcare cybersecurity roles = $140K-$180K with better hours than tech cybersecurity

**Cons:**
- ✗ Slower path to high earnings
- ✗ Healthcare IT may not be as exciting as general cybersecurity

## THE REAL TALK

### What I Actually Think (Step-by-Step Reasoning):

**Step 1**: Both are excellent choices (you can't go wrong)

**Step 2**: Age 28 starting point favors speed → Cybersecurity has advantage

**Step 3**: But 25-year career horizon (to age 53) favors work-life balance → NP has advantage

**Step 4**: Financial analysis: Cybersecurity wins (higher ROI, more money, faster)

**Step 5**: Lifestyle analysis: NP wins (better hours, zero on-call, more predictable)

**Step 6**: Risk analysis: Cybersecurity wins (lower investment, easier to pivot)

**Step 7**: Family considerations: NP wins (if you want kids/family time)

**Step 8**: The tie-breaker is: **What do you value more - Money or Time?**

### My Personal Recommendation:

**If you're single, no kids, and want to maximize wealth:**
→ **CYBERSECURITY** (work hard ages 30-45, build wealth, then coast or pivot)

**If you're married, have kids, or planning kids soon:**
→ **NURSE PRACTITIONER** (better work-life balance matters more when you have family)

**If you're unsure:**
→ **CYBERSECURITY** (lower risk, lower investment, can always go to NP school later at age 35-40)

### The Path I Would Choose (If I Were You):

**I would choose CYBERSECURITY because:**

1. **Time arbitrage**: At age 28, time is valuable. Getting employed by age 30 vs 33 is huge.

2. **Optionality**: $10K investment vs $70K investment. If I hate it, I'm only out 18 months and $10K.

3. **Financial runway**: Earning $100K+ by age 32 gives me options. I can:
   - Save for NP school later
   - Invest and build wealth
   - Take a sabbatical
   - Start a business
   - Geographic mobility

4. **Learning curve**: I can know if I like cybersecurity in 6 months. I can't know if I like being an NP until year 4-5 of education.

5. **Hedge**: If cybersecurity doesn't work out at age 30, I can STILL go to NP school at age 30-35. The reverse is harder (NP at 33, then try to learn cybersecurity at 38).

**BUT** - if I had kids or planned to have kids in next 5 years, I'd choose **NP** for the work-life balance.
