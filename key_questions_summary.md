# Key Questions Arising from Zero-to-Actuary Pathway Statement

## Primary Questions Identified

### 1. TIMELINE QUESTIONS

**Q: How long from zero math to UAlbany bachelor's degree?**
- **Answer**: 6-8 years full-time (2-4 years pre-college foundation + 4 years degree)
- **With actuarial credentialing**: 12-18 years total (ASA: +3-5 years, FSA: +3-5 more)
- **Within 25-year window**: Feasible but tight (degree by age 34-36, FSA by age 40-46)

**Q: Is the 25-year career window sufficient?**
- **Answer**: Yes, if starting immediately. Leaves 7-19 years of credentialed career post-FSA.

---

### 2. COST QUESTIONS

**Q: What is the total financial investment?**
- **Answer**: $200,000-$400,000 total (including $113,580-$222,440 direct costs + $120,000-$200,000 opportunity cost)
- **Break-even**: 3-4 years post-degree
- **ROI**: Positive within 25-year window IF successful

**Q: Is UAlbany the most cost-effective option?**
- **Answer**: No. Community college → transfer saves $70,000-$100,000. Consider alternatives.

---

### 3. AI DISRUPTION QUESTIONS

**Q: Will AI replace actuaries by 2050?**
- **Answer**: No. Private company analysis (McKinsey, Deloitte, PwC, Milliman) indicates:
  - 30-50% task automation (routine calculations)
  - Evolution toward advisory/strategic roles
  - Increased demand for AI-proficient actuaries
  - Regulatory requirements protect profession

**Q: Is starting in 2025 advantageous or disadvantageous?**
- **Answer**: Advantageous. Entering during AI integration means learning modern tools from start, not retraining.

---

### 4. JOB MARKET QUESTIONS

**Q: What is the job market outlook 2025-2050?**
- **Answer**: Strong. Private company reports (WTW, Aon, Mercer) indicate:
  - 20%+ growth projected 2024-2027
  - 30-40% of current actuaries retiring in 10-15 years
  - Strong demand for new entrants
  - Entry-level: $60,000-$80,000
  - Mid-career: $100,000-$150,000
  - Senior: $150,000-$300,000+

**Q: Are there alternative faster pathways?**
- **Answer**: Yes. Exam-first pathway (2 years to employment) vs. degree-first (6-8 years). Trade-off: faster entry vs. career ceiling.

---

### 5. PREREQUISITE QUESTIONS

**Q: What math level is needed before UAlbany?**
- **Answer**: High school math equivalent (Algebra I & II, Geometry, Pre-calculus, Trigonometry). This requires 2-4 years from "zero."

**Q: Can you skip foundational math?**
- **Answer**: No. Must build sequentially: arithmetic → algebra → calculus → statistics → actuarial math.

---

### 6. SUCCESS PROBABILITY QUESTIONS

**Q: What is the realistic success rate?**
- **Answer**: 60-70% complete credentialing. 30-40% attrition due to exam difficulty, career changes, life circumstances.

**Q: What if you fail actuarial exams?**
- **Answer**: Each retake costs $200-$1,000 + 3-6 months study. Some never pass despite years of effort. Significant risk.

---

### 7. OPPORTUNITY COST QUESTIONS

**Q: What are you giving up?**
- **Answer**: 
  - Time: 12-18 years of intense focus
  - Financial: $180,000-$400,000 in lost wages
  - Alternatives: Data science (6-12 months, $50,000-$100,000 start) or software engineering (6-12 months, $60,000-$120,000 start)

**Q: Is actuarial science "most favorable" compared to alternatives?**
- **Answer**: Depends on goals. Tech fields offer faster paths. Finance fields may offer higher ceilings. Actuarial offers stability and moderate-high earnings.

- **A**: 20-30 hours/week studying while working full-time. Affects relationships, hobbies, personal life.

### 12. AGE DISCRIMINATION
- **Q**: Is starting career at age 34-40 a disadvantage?
- **A**: Less than in tech fields. Actuarial field values credentials and experience. Age may be neutral or positive.

### 13. TECHNOLOGY LEARNING CURVE
- **Q**: Is math enough, or do you need tech skills too?
- **A**: Must learn Python/R, SQL, cloud platforms. Double learning curve (math + tech) if starting from zero.
