### CAREER PATH 1: REGULATORY MEDICAL WRITING

#### Overview
Regulatory medical writers create scientific and technical documents for pharmaceutical, biotechnology, and medical device companies. These documents are submitted to regulatory agencies (FDA, EMA, PMDA, etc.) for product approvals.

#### Core Documents Created
- Clinical Study Reports (CSRs)
- Investigator Brochures (IBs)
- Clinical Development Plans
- Regulatory submission modules (CTD format)
- Safety narratives
- Briefing documents
- Annual reports
- Risk management plans

### AI Impact Verification

**AI Risk Level**: LOW (10-20% job transformation, not replacement)

**Why Regulatory Writing Is AI-Resistant**:

1. **Regulatory Judgment Cannot Be Automated**
   - FDA guidelines require interpretation (not mechanical application)
   - Each drug/indication has unique considerations
   - Strategic decisions (what to emphasize, how to present data)
   - Example: How to present negative trial result while maintaining approvability
   - AI cannot make these judgment calls

2. **Legal Accountability Required**
   - Regulatory submissions are legal documents
   - Human must sign and take responsibility
   - FDA requires named, qualified individuals
   - AI cannot be legally liable

3. **Scientific Interpretation Is Complex**
   - Must understand clinical trial design, statistics, biology, pharmacology
   - Must synthesize data from multiple sources
   - Must identify patterns and inconsistencies
   - AI lacks integrated scientific reasoning

4. **Regulatory Agencies Won't Accept AI-Generated Documents**
   - FDA, EMA explicitly require human expertise
   - Quality standards are extremely high
   - Agencies audit documentation processes
   - AI cannot meet regulatory quality standards

5. **Contextual Nuance Is Critical**
   - Every patient population is unique
   - Every therapeutic area has specific conventions
   - Reviewer expectations vary by indication
   - Cultural sensitivity (global submissions)
   - AI cannot capture this nuance

**How AI Will Help (Positive Transformation)**:
- **Grammar/spell check**: Enhanced editing tools
- **Formatting**: Automated table of contents, reference formatting
- **Data extraction**: Pull data from databases faster
- **Translation**: Multi-regional submission support
