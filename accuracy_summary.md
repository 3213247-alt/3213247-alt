## Critical Flaws in Original Analysis

### 1. **Attrition Rate Methodology**
**Problem:** Assumes linear progression through exams
- Assumes: P → FM → Exam 3 → Exam 4 (in order)
- Reality: Many candidates:
  - Retake failed exams
  - Take exams out of order
  - Switch between SOA and CAS tracks
  - Enter mid-career with some exams already passed

**Impact:** Likely **overestimates attrition** by 20-40%
- If attrition is actually lower, more candidates reach 3-4 exams than calculated
- Final candidate count (80-100) could be significantly **underestimated**

### 2. **Arbitrary Skill Percentages**
**Problem:** No data source for:
- 70% have tech skills
- 80% have communication skills

**Impact:** These appear to be **guesses**, not data-driven
- Could be higher or lower
- Affects final candidate count calculation

### 3. **Age Success Rates**
**Problem:** No actuarial-specific data
- 70-80% vs 20-30% appears to be **inference** from general labor market
- No actuarial career outcome studies found

**Impact:** Numbers are **opinion-based**, not data-based
- Directionally likely correct (age matters)
- Specific percentages cannot be verified
