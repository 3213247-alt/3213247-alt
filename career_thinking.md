# Career Comparison: Regulatory Medical Writing vs. Actuary

## 1. Entry Barrier at Age 28 (BS, UAlbany)
- **Actuary:**
    - **Requirement:** Bachelors + 2-3 Exams passed (P, FM).
    - **Feasibility:** High. UAlbany has solid math programs.
    - **Time to Entry:** 6-12 months (study time for exams).
    - **Barrier:** Intellectual difficulty of exams. 50% pass rates.
- **Regulatory Medical Writer (RMW):**
    - **Requirement:** Usually PhD/PharmD. BS requires 2-5 years related industry experience (e.g., Clinical Research Associate).
    - **Feasibility:** Low for *direct* entry as "Medical Writer". Moderate if starting in adjacent role.
    - **Time to Entry:** Immediate for adjacent roles, 3-5 years to transition to RMW.
    - **Barrier:** "Degree inflation" and experience paradox (need experience to get job).

## 2. The Mobility Squeeze (Geography & Economy)
- **Actuary:**
    - **Constraint:** Jurisdiction-specific. US credentials (SOA/CAS) are prestigious but legal frameworks (Statutory Accounting Principles) are US-centric.
    - **Risk:** Tied to US GDP and US Insurance market health. Hard to practice in EU/Asia without re-certification or demotion.
- **RMW:**
    - **Constraint:** Harmonized. ICH-GCP guidelines are global standards (US, EU, Japan, Canada).
    - **Benefit:** Skillset is globally portable. A Protocol or CSR is standard worldwide.
    - **Hedge:** If US economy collapses, Pharma R&D may shift, but the *writer* can contract for foreign companies from anywhere or relocate.

## 3. The 40-Year Future (Automation & Demand)
- **Actuary:**
    - **Trend:** Data processing automated. Risk interpretation remains.
    - **Demand:** Climate change and cyber risk increase demand for novel risk modeling.
    - **Stability:** High. Insurance is legally mandated.
- **RMW:**
    - **Trend:** Generative AI writes the first draft. Role shifts to QC, strategy, and compliance logic.
    - **Demand:** Drug approval volume increases with aging population.
    - **Instability:** Biotech is interest-rate sensitive. High layoff frequency in biotech downturns.

## 4. The Critical "Squeeze" Result
- **The Trade-off:** 
    - Actuary trades **freedom of movement** for **guaranteed local stability**.
    - RMW trades **job security** for **freedom of location**.
- **The Winner for "Life Long Term" (20+ years):**
    - If prediction is "US Decline": **RMW** wins (portability).
    - If prediction is "Global Depression": **Actuary** wins (Insurance is mandatory, new drug R&D is discretionary).

## 5. Economic Depression Prediction
- **Frequency:** Major recessions every 10-15 years. True "Depressions" are rare (1929).
- **Outlook:** Structural inflation and debt cycles suggest volatility.
- **Winner in Depression:** Actuary. People stop developing drugs; they do not stop insuring houses/cars (legally required) or health (necessity).

## 6. UAlbany Context
- UAlbany Great Danes: Strong ROI for NY residents.
- Leverage Math dept for Actuary.
- Leverage Biology/English for RMW (but BS is weak leverage).
