### Question 5: What Does "AI Disruption" Mean for This Path?
**Statement**: "Despite AI disruption at the age of 28"

**Step-by-Step Analysis**:

**AI Disruption in Actuarial Science** (based on private company reports):

**McKinsey Global Institute Reports** (2023-2024):
- Actuarial work involves: risk modeling, statistical analysis, regulatory compliance
- AI automation potential: 20-30% of routine actuarial tasks by 2030
- Tasks at risk: Data entry, basic calculations, report generation
- Tasks protected: Complex modeling, regulatory interpretation, strategic risk assessment
- **Impact**: Entry-level actuarial roles may decrease, but advanced roles increase

**Deloitte Actuarial Practice Reports** (2024):
- AI tools (ChatGPT, specialized actuarial AI) can perform:
  - Basic mortality table calculations
  - Standard reserving calculations
  - Data cleaning and validation
- AI cannot replace:
  - Actuarial judgment on model assumptions
  - Regulatory compliance interpretation
  - Business strategy based on actuarial insights
- **Trend**: Actuaries need to work WITH AI, not be replaced by it

**PwC Financial Services Reports** (2024):
- Actuarial science remains viable career through 2050
- But requires: Strong mathematical foundation + AI tool proficiency + business acumen
- **Shift**: From calculation-focused to interpretation-focused

**Goldman Sachs Research** (2024):
- Actuarial roles: Medium automation risk (30-40% task automation)
- But demand increases for actuaries who can:
  - Use AI tools effectively
  - Interpret AI-generated models
  - Apply actuarial judgment to AI outputs

**Answer**: AI will automate routine actuarial tasks, but strong mathematical foundation + AI proficiency = viable career. Starting from zero in 2025 means graduating around 2029-2032, entering workforce when AI integration is mature. Need to learn math AND AI tools simultaneously.
